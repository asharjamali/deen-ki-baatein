Entering Home Dua

Bless your home with Allah’s name

About Entering Home Dua

The Entering Home Dua is recited when entering one’s home to seek Allah’s blessings and protection for the household. It reflects gratitude and reliance on Allah for safety and provision.

Text and Meaning

Arabic: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا
Transliteration: Bismillahi walajna wa bismillahi kharajna
Translation: In the name of Allah we enter, and in the name of Allah we leave.

When to Recite

Recite when entering the home, ideally stepping in with the right foot, to invoke blessings and protection.

Benefits

Brings blessings to the household, protects from evil, and fosters a peaceful home environment.

Key Supplication

Entering Home Dua
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا
Bismillahi walajna wa bismillahi kharajna
In the name of Allah we enter, and in the name of Allah we leave.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud, Book 41, Hadith 5096

This Dua invokes Allah’s name upon entering and leaving the home, ensuring the household is blessed and protected. It is a Sunnah to recite it with the right foot forward when entering.
