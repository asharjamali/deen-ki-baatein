Morning Dua

Start your day with Allah’s protection and blessings

About Morning Dua

The Morning Dua is a powerful supplication recited at the start of the day to seek Allah’s protection, guidance, and blessings. It is part of the Adhkar (remembrances) recommended to strengthen one’s faith and begin the day with mindfulness.

Text and Meaning

Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا
Transliteration: Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna
Translation: O Allah, by You we enter the morning and by You we enter the evening.

When to Recite

Recite after Fajr prayer or at the start of the day to seek protection and blessings for the day ahead.

Benefits

Invokes Allah’s protection from harm, strengthens faith, and brings tranquility to start the day.

Key Supplication

Morning Dua for Protection
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا
Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna
O Allah, by You we enter the morning and by You we enter the evening.
Reference: Sahih Muslim, Book 35, Hadith 6570

This Dua is recited to acknowledge Allah’s sovereignty over the day and night, seeking His protection and blessings. It is a reminder of reliance on Allah for safety and guidance throughout the day.
