Dua for Morning

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا

Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna

Dua of the Day

Dua for Forgiveness
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَإِسْرَافَنَا فِي أَمْرِنَا
Rabbana ighfir lana dhunubana wa israfana fi amrina
Our Lord, forgive us our sins and our extravagance in our affairs.
Reference: Surah Aal-E-Imran [3:147]

This powerful Dua seeks Allah’s forgiveness for sins and excesses, reflecting humility and repentance. It is recommended to recite this supplication regularly to seek mercy and purification of the heart.
