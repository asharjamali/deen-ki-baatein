Ruling of the Day

Purification (Wudu)
Obligation of Wudu before Salah
Reference: Surah Al-Ma’idah [5:6]

Wudu is a mandatory ritual purification required before performing Salah. It involves washing specific body parts in a prescribed order, ensuring spiritual and physical cleanliness, as commanded in the Quran.
