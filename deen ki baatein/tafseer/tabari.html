Imam Ibn Jarir At-Tabari

Muhammad ibn Jarir al-Tabari (رحمه الله) was a giant among early Muslim scholars. His monumental work Jāmiʿ al-Bayān ʿan Taʾwīl Āy al-Qurʾān is the first major comprehensive Tafseer. It collects opinions from Sahaba, Tabi’een and early Imams with full chains of narration (isnad).
