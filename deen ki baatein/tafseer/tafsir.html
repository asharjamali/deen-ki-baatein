Verse of the Day

وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ
"And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me."
Surah Adh-Dhariyat [51:56]

This verse emphasizes the purpose of human creation: to worship Allah alone, highlighting devotion and submission as the core of existence.
