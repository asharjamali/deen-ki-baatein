About Tafseer Ibn Kathir

Tafseer Ibn Kathir, authored by Ismail ibn Umar ibn Kathir (1300–1373 CE), is one of the most renowned and comprehensive Quranic exegeses. It combines explanations from the Quran, Hadith, and statements of the Sahaba, providing deep historical and linguistic insights.

Key Features

Focuses on authentic narrations, detailed historical context, and linguistic analysis of Quranic verses.

Historical Context

Provides stories of prophets and past nations to enrich understanding of Quranic narratives.

Example Tafseer

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ
"Allah - there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence."
Surah Al-Baqarah [2:255]

Ibn Kathir explains that this verse, part of Ayat al-Kursi, emphasizes Allah’s sole divinity, His eternal life, and His role as the sustainer of all creation. He cites Hadith that highlight its protective virtues when recited.
